Research prototype

PapVision

Cervical cytology field classifier — MobileNetV3-Small, four Bethesda classes.

⚠ Not a medical device · Not for diagnostic use

This model has been evaluated, and it fails. Cross-validated recall on squamous cell carcinoma is 0.15 — it misses roughly six carcinoma fields in seven, usually calling them HSIL with high confidence. A logistic regression on six colour numbers scores better. It is not calibrated and has never been validated on clinical slides.

It is not a substitute for examination by a qualified cytopathologist. Do not use any output here to make, support, or influence a clinical decision. If you have a health concern, talk to a doctor.
